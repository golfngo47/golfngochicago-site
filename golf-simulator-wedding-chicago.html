Why Golf 'n Go for Weddings

Elegant Setup.
Every Guest Involved.

The cocktail hour is the hardest part of a wedding to manage — guests are mingling, but there's often a lull where people aren't sure what to do. A golf simulator solves that instantly. It creates a natural gathering point, sparks conversation across family groups and friend circles, and gives guests something genuinely fun and interactive while the wedding party takes photos.

We coordinate with your venue, arrive early, set up quietly, and operate through the duration of your cocktail hour or reception. When the experience wraps, we break down cleanly and quickly. Your wedding day timeline stays intact — we work around you, not the other way around.

Golfers and non-golfers both love it. Our attendant guides every guest through the experience — from the grandfather who plays every weekend to the maid of honor who's never held a club. With 2,300+ courses on GSPro, we can even feature the couple's favorite course as a personal touch.
