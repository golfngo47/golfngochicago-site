Why Golf 'n Go for Fundraisers

Engagement That
Drives Giving.

The most effective fundraisers give donors something to do — not just something to watch. A golf simulator creates a competitive, energetic centerpiece that draws people in, keeps them engaged all evening, and ties directly to your fundraising goals through paid entries, pledge-per-shot formats, and Hole in One prize contests.

We bring the Flightscope Mevo+ launch monitor, GSPro software with 2,300+ courses, 4K projector, screen or inflatable enclosure, clubs, and balls. Our on-site attendant runs every rotation and keeps the leaderboard live so the competition stays tight and visible — which keeps donors around longer and giving more.

Works beautifully at galas, banquets, outdoor fundraisers, golf scrambles, school events, and nonprofit benefit nights. We set up before doors open and break down after the last guest leaves so your team stays focused entirely on the mission.
