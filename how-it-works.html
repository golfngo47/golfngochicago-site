Quick Answers

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

How much space do you need?

Our inflatable enclosure needs about 20×20 ft with 11 ft of ceiling height. Our compact indoor setup fits in just 15×10 ft with 9 ft ceilings. Both deliver the same full experience.

Do you need power on site?

Yes — we need access to a standard power outlet. We handle all cabling and setup so you don't have to worry about a thing.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 3–4 weeks minimum to ensure your date is available. Ideally 8–12 weeks out is best — dates are first come, first served.

Can total beginners play?

Absolutely. The simulator works for all skill levels — we've had guests who've never picked up a club having just as much fun as scratch golfers.

What's the deposit policy?

We require a 25% deposit to secure your date. The remaining balance is due the day of the event. We accept Zelle, check, and card.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Orland Park and serve 100+ communities across Chicagoland. A $50 travel fee applies to events more than 25 miles from Orland Park.
