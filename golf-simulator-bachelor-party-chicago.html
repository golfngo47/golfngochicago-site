Competition Formats

Pick Your Battle. We'll Run the Scoreboard.

Our attendant sets up and manages whichever format fits your crew. Mix and match for longer bookings — or let us call it based on your group.

🎯 Closest to the Pin

Everyone targets the same approach shot. Whoever lands closest to the flag wins the hole — and the bragging rights. No golf skill required to compete.

💥 Longest Drive

Pure power. One tee, one shot, one winner. Great as an opener — sets the energy early and gets everyone fired up to swing hard.

⚔️ Match Play Head-to-Head

Bracket-style 1v1 battles across 9 holes. The crew watches, heckles, and picks sides. Winner advances. Loser owes a round.

🤝 Worst-Ball Scramble

Teams play every shot from the worst lie in the group. High-stakes chaos that makes even bad golfers absolutely essential — sometimes on purpose.

🏆 Custom Tournament

Multiple rounds, rotating formats, live leaderboard. Best for 5hr+ bookings when you want the simulator to be the main event of the night.

🎮 Just Play a Round

No competition needed — just put on Pebble Beach, grab a drink, and play golf with the guys. Relaxed, scenic, and still more memorable than a bar.
