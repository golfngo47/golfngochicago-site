Why Golf 'n Go

The Entertainment
Nobody Forgets.

Private parties are better when there's something everyone can do together. Golf 'n Go turns any backyard, living room, garage, or event space into a fully functional golf entertainment venue — no course required, no membership needed, no experience necessary. We show up before your guests, set everything up, and run the entire experience from start to finish so you can actually enjoy the gathering you planned.

The simulator works for every kind of private party because it works for every kind of person. Kids love seeing their shots launch across a giant screen. Competitive adults turn it into a tournament. Non-golfers find it instantly fun — the Hole in One Challenge, Long Drive Contest, and arcade mini-games are designed for people who've never held a club. Serious golfers get to play Pebble Beach or Augusta National from your backyard.

We bring the Flightscope Mevo+ launch monitor, GSPro software with 2,300+ world-famous courses, 4K projector, screen or inflatable enclosure, clubs, and balls. Everything is included. You provide the guests — we provide the reason they can't stop talking about the party.

What Sets It Apart

More Than Just Entertainment.

The simulator is entertaining even if you've never swung a club. Our on-site attendant makes it approachable for total beginners while keeping it challenging for guests who play regularly. Everyone plays, everyone competes, everyone laughs.

Built-In Competition

Long Drive Challenges, Closest to the Pin, Hole in One contests, and arcade mini-games turn any gathering into a tournament. The simulator creates a natural gathering point where people rotate in, cheer each other on, and compete for bragging rights.

Completely Turnkey
