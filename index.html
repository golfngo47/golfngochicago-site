Got Questions

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before booking.

We offer two enclosure options depending on your event space. Our inflatable enclosure — ideal for outdoor events and larger indoor venues — requires approximately 20×20 feet of space with at least 11 feet of ceiling height. Our compact indoor enclosure is perfect for tighter spaces and requires just 15×10 feet with at least 9 feet of ceiling height. Both setups deliver the exact same simulator experience — same software, same courses, same gameplay. Just let us know your venue details when you book and we'll bring the right setup for your space.

We recommend booking three to four weeks out to ensure your date is available. Ideally, eight to twelve weeks in advance is best — it's first come, first served for date and time.

Everything. We bring the full simulator setup including the projector, screen, mat, clubs, balls, and software. We handle delivery, setup (45–60 min), operation during your event, and full teardown afterward. You don't need to supply anything.

We serve 100+ communities across Chicagoland — from Waukegan to Kankakee and Elgin to Indiana. If you're within roughly 100 miles of Orland Park, there's a strong chance we can make it work. Search your city in our Service Areas section or give us a call at (708) 264-4980 to confirm.

We require a 25% deposit to secure your date, with the remaining balance due the day of the event. We accept Zelle, check, and card payments.
